Drop, cover, and hold

Protect your body from falling debris and structural hazards during active ground shaking.

DROP to hands and knees immediately
COVER your head and neck under a sturdy desk, table, or against an interior wall
HOLD ON to your shelter and be prepared to move with it until shaking stops
IF outdoors: move away from buildings, streetlights, and utility wires and drop to the ground
IF in a vehicle: pull over away from buildings, trees, and overpasses and stay inside
Stay in position until all shaking has completely stopped

Assess immediate safety hazards

Determine whether your immediate environment is safe to remain in or requires evacuation.

Check yourself for injuries before moving
Inspect the room for structural damage, cracks in walls, or ceiling collapse
Sniff for gas leaks — IF detected: do not operate any switches, evacuate immediately, and call 911 from outside
Check for sparking electrical wires or signs of fire
Inspect for water line breaks or flooding
IF imminent structural collapse risk detected: evacuate the building immediately
Wear closed-toe shoes before moving through debris

Execute PACE check-in

Confirm your status to your designated out-of-area contact and execute your communication plan.

Open PACE Communication Plan
Attempt contact via Primary channel (e.g., cell call)
IF no response or network congestion: switch to Alternate channel (e.g., text message, which uses less bandwidth)
IF Alternate fails: use Contingency channel (e.g., messaging app over Wi-Fi)
IF all digital channels fail: use Emergency channel (e.g., in-person at designated meeting point)
Contact your pre-designated out-of-area contact to confirm your status and location
Use your offline-stored contact list if phone memory is inaccessible
Log the time and result of each contact attempt

Evaluate evacuation triggers

Determine whether to shelter in place or evacuate based on your pre-defined trigger conditions.

Open Evacuation Route plan
Check for official evacuation orders via battery-powered radio or emergency alerts
Assess structural integrity of your building — IF unsafe: trigger evacuation
Check for active fire, flooding, or gas leak nearby — IF present: trigger evacuation
IF evacuating: grab go-bag, critical documents, and follow your primary evacuation route
IF sheltering in place: move to the safest interior room away from windows
Identify your two pre-named evacuation destinations in materially different directions
Monitor for aftershocks and reassess shelter-in-place decision after each significant tremor

Retrieve go-bag and documents

Secure all critical supplies and vital documents needed to sustain operations during or after evacuation.

Pull up Go Bag packing list to confirm contents
Retrieve go-bag from its stored location
Retrieve vital documents (ID, passport, insurance policies, medical records) from stored location
Confirm emergency cash buffer is included in your go-bag
Verify your offline copy of the emergency plan is in the bag
IF evacuation is imminent: add any prescription medications not already packed
Confirm go-bag weight is manageable for solo carry over distance

Activate shelter-in-place resources

Access your pre-positioned supplies to sustain yourself for at least 72 hours without outside assistance.

Open your inventory list and verify available supplies
Locate your 72-hour water supply (minimum 3 gallons for 1 person)
IF tap water is unsafe or cut off: use your stored supply and activate your water filtration method
Locate your 72-hour food supply and account for any dietary or medical food needs
Execute your power-out plan — activate flashlights, battery banks, or alternate light source
Locate and confirm access to any prescription medications and medical equipment
IF on critical medication: contact your backup pharmacy and doctor using your offline contact list
Tune battery-powered radio to local emergency broadcast for official updates

Document and report damage

Record all property damage and report relevant hazards to support insurance claims and community safety.

Photograph or video all visible structural damage, room by room
Document damage to appliances, utilities, and personal property
Reference your home inventory documentation to cross-check affected items
Report gas leaks, downed power lines, or structural hazards to local utility or emergency services
Note the time and nature of any aftershocks experienced
Contact your insurance provider to initiate a claim as soon as communications allow
Avoid entering damaged structures until officially cleared by authorities

Confirm plan accessibility and review

Verify that your emergency plan is accessible and up to date for future events.

Confirm your offline copy of the full emergency plan is intact and legible
Notify your trusted external contact that the emergency has occurred and that you are safe
Review which steps in this plan worked well and which need updating
Check if your PACE plan drill cadence is current — IF more than 6 months since last drill: schedule a review
Replenish any supplies consumed during this event
Update your out-of-area contact information if any details have changed
Log lessons learned and incorporate into updated plan

Take immediate protective action

Protect yourself from falling debris and structural collapse during active shaking.

DROP to hands and knees immediately
COVER your head and neck with your arms or crawl under a sturdy table
HOLD ON to your shelter until shaking stops
IF outdoors: move away from buildings, trees, and power lines and stay low
IF in a vehicle: pull over away from overpasses and bridges and stay inside
IF in bed: stay there and cover your head and neck with a pillow
Stay in position until all shaking has fully stopped

Assess immediate safety hazards

Determine whether your environment is safe to remain in or requires immediate evacuation.

Check yourself for injuries before moving
Inspect your surroundings for structural damage such as cracks, collapsed walls, or shifted foundations
Smell for gas leaks and listen for hissing sounds near gas lines
IF gas leak detected: do not use any electrical switches, exit immediately, and call your gas company from outside
Check for downed power lines near your exit paths
Inspect for fire or smoke originating inside or near the building
IF imminent structural collapse, fire, or gas leak detected: evacuate immediately and call 911

Execute PACE check-in

Confirm your status and location with your designated contacts through your communication channels.

Open PACE Communication Plan
Attempt contact via Primary channel (e.g., cell call or text to designated contact)
IF no response within 15 minutes: switch to Alternate channel (e.g., messaging app or social media check-in)
IF Alternate fails: proceed to Contingency channel (e.g., landline, neighbor relay)
Contact your designated out-of-area contact to confirm your status
Use your offline-stored copy of critical contact info if cell networks are overloaded
Log the time and outcome of each contact attempt

Retrieve vital documents

Secure your critical personal and financial documents from their designated storage location.

Retrieve your vital documents from their pre-designated storage location (ID, passport, insurance policies, medical records)
Confirm your emergency cash buffer is accessible for when card systems are down
Reference your insurance policies and home inventory documentation
IF documents are inaccessible due to structural damage: do not re-enter an unsafe structure
Place all retrieved documents in a waterproof bag or pouch
Keep documents on your person or in your go bag

Evaluate evacuation or shelter decision

Determine whether to shelter in place or evacuate based on your pre-defined triggers and current conditions.

Open Evacuation Route
Assess your building for structural integrity before deciding to stay
IF structural damage is visible, gas is leaking, or authorities order evacuation: activate your evacuation plan
IF building is structurally sound and no immediate hazards exist: prepare to shelter in place
Monitor local emergency broadcasts via battery-powered or hand-crank radio for official guidance
Act on your pre-defined evacuation triggers (e.g., visible structural damage, official evacuation order, loss of utilities)

Grab go bag and evacuate

Exit your location safely with all essential supplies if evacuation has been triggered.

Pull up Go Bag packing list to confirm contents before departing
Retrieve your pre-packed go bag (inspected within the last 12 months)
Put on sturdy closed-toe shoes to protect feet from debris
Follow your primary pre-determined evacuation route
IF primary route is blocked by debris or damage: switch to your alternate evacuation route
Travel to your pre-named near-home meeting location or your farther evacuation destination
Avoid damaged roads, bridges, and areas near coastlines (tsunami risk after major earthquakes)

Activate shelter-in-place plan

Sustain yourself safely using your prepared supplies if remaining at your location is the safest option.

Open Inventory List to locate and verify all shelter-in-place supplies
Access your 72-hour water supply (minimum 1 gallon per day for 1 person = 3 gallons minimum)
Identify your water extension method (filter, purification tablets, or identified backup source) in case supply runs low
Access your 72-hour food supply and confirm no items require cooking if gas or power is out
Activate your power-out plan: deploy your battery-powered light source and maintain device charging via power bank
Maintain medical continuity by accessing your prescription buffer supply and any required medical equipment
IF prescription supply is running low: contact your backup pharmacy using your offline contact info
Monitor battery-powered or hand-crank radio for aftershock warnings and official updates

Document damage and begin recovery

Record all property damage and initiate your insurance and financial recovery process.

Photograph or video all visible structural and property damage before any cleanup begins
Record the date, time, and description of damages for your insurance claim
Contact your insurance provider using your offline-stored policy and contact information
IF utilities are compromised: report outages to your electric, gas, and water utilities using offline-stored numbers
Do not re-enter a structurally unsafe building until cleared by a certified inspector
Identify local emergency resources, shelters, and community support centers via radio or posted notices
Confirm your out-of-area contact is updated on your recovery status and current location

Assess immediate safety hazards

Determine whether your immediate environment is safe to remain in or requires evacuation.

Drop, cover, and hold on until all shaking stops completely
Check your body for injuries before moving
Inspect walls, ceiling, and floor for visible structural damage
Sniff for gas leaks and listen for hissing sounds near gas lines
Check for sparking wires or electrical hazards
IF structural collapse is visible or imminent: exit the building immediately
IF gas leak is detected: leave the building, leave the door open, and do not use any switches or flames
Call 911 if you are injured or trapped

Account for medical needs

Ensure your medical continuity is maintained immediately following the earthquake.

Locate your prescription medications and confirm you have at least a 7-day supply
Check any medical equipment (CPAP, insulin pump, etc.) for damage
IF medication supply is low: contact your backup pharmacy using your stored contact list
IF medical equipment is damaged: contact your equipment provider or emergency services
Take any required medications on schedule
Document any injury symptoms that may worsen and require medical attention

Execute PACE check-in

Confirm your status and location with your designated contacts through established communication channels.

Open PACE Communication Plan
Attempt contact via Primary channel (e.g., cell phone call)
IF no response within 15 minutes: switch to Alternate channel (e.g., SMS text message)
IF Alternate fails: proceed to Contingency channel (e.g., social media check-in or messaging app)
IF all digital channels fail: proceed to Emergency channel (e.g., in-person rally point or landline)
Contact your designated out-of-area contact to report your status
Use your printed or offline-stored contact list if cell networks are down
Log the time and outcome of each contact attempt

Evaluate evacuation triggers

Decide whether to evacuate or shelter in place based on your pre-defined triggers and current conditions.

Open Evacuation Route plan
Check for official evacuation orders via battery-powered radio or emergency alerts
Assess structural integrity of your building for safe habitation
IF official evacuation order issued: begin immediate evacuation
IF building is structurally compromised: begin immediate evacuation
IF no evacuation order and building is intact: initiate shelter-in-place protocol
Identify your two pre-named evacuation destinations in materially different directions
Confirm your primary evacuation route is passable and identify your alternate route if blocked

Grab go bag and evacuate

Depart with all essential items if evacuation has been determined necessary.

Pull up Go Bag packing list to verify contents before departure
Retrieve your go bag from its stored location
Confirm go bag contains water, food, first aid kit, and flashlight
Retrieve your vital documents (ID, passport, insurance, medical records) from their stored location
Grab your emergency cash buffer
Put on sturdy, closed-toe shoes before exiting
Travel to your primary evacuation destination
IF primary route is blocked by debris or damage: take your alternate evacuation route

Activate shelter-in-place protocol

Sustain yourself safely using your stored supplies if remaining in place is the appropriate decision.

Locate your stored water supply and confirm you have at least 3 gallons (1 gal/day for 3 days) for 1 person
IF tap water safety is unknown: do not drink unconfirmed tap water and use stored supply only
Identify your water extension method (filter, purification tablets, or known safe secondary source)
Access your 72-hour food supply and account for any dietary restrictions
Activate your power-out plan: locate flashlights, battery banks, and backup lighting
IF temperature is a concern: implement your warmth or cooling strategy appropriate to your climate
Monitor emergency broadcasts on your battery-powered radio for updates
Ration water and food consumption to ensure 72-hour supply lasts

Secure and document property damage

Stabilize your property and document damage for insurance and recovery purposes.

Walk through each room and photograph all visible damage
Record a video walkthrough of the entire property interior and exterior
Shut off the main gas valve if a gas leak was detected or suspected
Shut off electricity at the main breaker if electrical hazards are present
IF water lines are damaged: shut off the main water supply valve
Do not re-enter any room with ceiling or wall cracks that suggest structural failure
Note and document all damaged items with descriptions and estimated values
Contact your insurance provider using your stored policy information to report the claim

Review critical documents and finances

Confirm access to your vital records and financial resources needed for recovery.

Verify your vital documents are accessible and undamaged (ID, passport, insurance policies, deeds, medical records)
Review your insurance policy documents to understand coverage for earthquake damage
Access your emergency cash buffer for immediate needs if card systems are down
IF documents were damaged or lost: contact issuing agencies to begin replacement
Log all emergency-related expenses starting from the date of the earthquake
Confirm your homeowner's or renter's insurance contact number is in your offline contact list

Confirm household plan accessibility

Verify your emergency plan remains accessible and actionable for ongoing recovery operations.

Open Inventory List to verify current supply levels after use
Restock any supplies consumed during the emergency response (water, food, first aid items)
Confirm your offline copy of the plan (printed binder or offline app cache) is intact and accessible
Notify your trusted external contact that you have the plan and your current status
Schedule a post-incident review to update the plan based on lessons learned
Note any gaps in preparedness discovered during this emergency for future improvement
